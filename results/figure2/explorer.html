A first look · about 5 minutes

A neck model, one idea at a time.

This repo asks: how hard can a modeled head push against resistance while staying still? Then it changes one group of neck muscles and asks again.

Biomechanics means using forces and motion to understand bodies. Start with the picture; the scientific names can come afterward.

Go deeper: how much of each muscle group is used?

Activation is the fraction of a group’s available capacity used in a solution: 0 means none; 1 means all of it.

Each setting is shown at its own maximum push. The bars are one solution chosen by the calculation; other equally good solutions may exist. A group with zero capacity contributes nothing, whatever its displayed activation.

Research details, source comparison and diagnostics

What was reproduced?

Abbott et al. (2024), Figure 2: a fixed, neutral head posture, 3 modeled joints, 9 rotational coordinates, and 96 muscle elements sharing 24 group activations. The solver finds the largest contact force whose turning effects can be balanced at every joint coordinate.

The supplied model object agrees with the supplement’s rounded muscle parameters. The ancillary KinModels.mat export has different lower-joint parameters and is not used for this baseline. The stored anatomical grouping also places splenius cervicis with levator scapulae, unlike Supplement Table S3; this implementation retains the actual stored grouping.

Three backward-pushing tests in the side-view panel have small 1×→2× gains, despite the paper’s broad statement that doubling does not increase strength. See the reproduction report for measured values and limitations.

This static formulation excludes gravity compensation, device support, fatigue, pain, disc loading and dynamics. It uses maximum isometric capacities with fixed moment arms and coupled group activations.

A little more intuition: joints, torque and moment arms
A joint is a connection where body parts can move relative to each other. This model simplifies the neck to three joints, each with three ways of turning: nodding, tilting sideways and turning left/right. That is what “9 degrees of freedom” means here, even though all are held still during each test.
Torque is a force’s turning effect. Think of opening a door: the same push turns it more effectively at the handle than close to the hinge. The moment arm is the perpendicular distance from the turning axis to the force’s line of action. Muscles have different pulling directions and moment arms, so more strength in one group does not necessarily increase the whole team’s maximum push.
